The list you actually need isn't in any database.

Every prospecting tool works the same way: pick an industry, pick a headcount, pick a location, export. But the thing that makes a company a good fit for you is almost never one of those three fields. It's something you'd have to open their website to know.

So you compromise. You export the broad filter, get 4,000 rows, and either mail all of them — which is why reply rates are what they are — or spend your week manually checking which ones are real.

An agent doesn't have that limit. It can open every company's site and check the thing you actually care about, one at a time, at a scale you can't.

Asks no filter can answer

These are the kinds of requests the agent is built for. None of them are a checkbox in Apollo or ZoomInfo.

💍

"Wedding venues running more than one property that still take bookings by email."

Requires reading the venue's site and booking flow. There's no "still uses email" field anywhere.

🏢

"Insurance agencies where the owner is near retirement and hasn't hired in two years."

Requires cross-referencing licensing records, LinkedIn tenure, and job postings that stopped.

💰

"SaaS companies that raised seed in the last 90 days and just posted their first sales role."

Two signals from two sources, joined. The window is weeks wide and no static list catches it.

🔧

"Manufacturers whose site still lists a fax number and has no online quoting."

A proxy for how much they'd benefit from what you sell — and readable only by looking.
